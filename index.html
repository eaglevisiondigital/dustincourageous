Book #1 · The Adventure Begins

What Is The No-Fear Secret™ About?

Dustin has a big secret: fear tries to stop him, hold him back, and make him believe the lies of the enemy. But Dustin begins to discover an even greater truth—fear is not from God, and God has given him power, love, and a sound mind.

Built around 2 Timothy 1:7 and 1 John 4:4, this faith-filled adventure helps kids understand that the Greater One lives in them. As Dustin learns to stand strong, speak God's truth, and refuse to let fear have the final word, young readers are reminded that they can face life's challenges knowing who they are and what they have in Christ.

2 Timothy 1:7Power, love, and a sound mind.1 John 4:4The Greater One is in you.
Book #2 · The Adventure Continues

What Is The BIG Things Miracle™ About?

When Dustin learns that the Johnson family needs nearly $10,000 for a new air conditioner, he knows he wants to help. With his friends, a BIG idea, and a whole lot of faith, the kids start a lemonade stand—but when things begin slowly, Dustin learns to pray, listen, and obey the next idea God puts in his heart.

What happens next becomes bigger than they could have imagined. Rooted in Philippians 4:13 and Ephesians 3:20, this adventure shows kids that God gives good ideas, obedience leads to the next step, and little kids can make a big difference when they trust a BIG God.

Philippians 4:13I can do all things through Christ.Ephesians 3:20God is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think.
