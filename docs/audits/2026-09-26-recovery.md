guardian approval/revocation, protected XP/badge awards, child-household
entitlements and admin separation are preserved. Private tables remain directly
inaccessible to clients, and raw XP/badge helpers remain owner-only. Full catalog
comparison catches unintended changes to permissions or function bodies.

The [legacy grant inventory](../recovery/LEGACY_GRANTS.md) explicitly documents

broad view privileges, six broad default ACL records and seven implicit PUBLIC

approved as a launch policy. RLS does not govern TRUNCATE. No sweeping grant
cleanup or client privilege expansion was performed.

The [Edge audit](../recovery/EDGE_FUNCTION_AUDIT.md) covers each recovered

access, CORS, validation, secret handling, errors and usage evidence. Legacy

acceptance of the provider-test endpoint. No newly confirmed exploitable
unauthenticated authorization bypass justified changing deployed behavior here.
